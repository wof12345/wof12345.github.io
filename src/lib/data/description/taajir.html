Taajir is an all-in-one inventory and business management platform built for the shops that keep a high street running — the corner electronics store, the wholesale supplier, the growing multi-branch retailer.

Most small and medium businesses run on a patchwork of spreadsheets, paper notebooks, and half a dozen apps that never quite talk to each other. Stock counts drift, invoices get lost, and by the time the numbers are added up at month-end, the moment to act has already passed. Taajir was built to close that gap — a single system that follows a product from the moment it arrives in the stockroom to the moment it's handed across the counter.

One source of truth, from the stockroom to the storefront.

At its core is an inventory engine that understands how real shops actually work. Products can carry variants, serial numbers, barcodes, and custom fields, and they can be sold in whatever unit makes sense — by the piece, the box, or the kilogram — with conversions handled automatically. Every inbound, outbound, transfer, and adjustment is recorded, so stock levels stay honest and low-stock alerts fire before a shelf ever goes empty.

The point of sale is designed for speed at the busiest part of the day. Cashiers ring up sales, generate quotations, and process returns in a few taps, while invoicing handles VAT, discounts, and multiple templates without a second thought. Purchasing closes the loop on the other side — supplier records, purchase invoices, and returns all feed straight back into the same live stock figures.

Underneath the day-to-day sits the part owners care about most: the money. A real-time ledger tracks every cash flow across cash, bank, card, and mobile payment accounts, expenses are categorised as they happen, and dashboards turn it all into a clear picture of sales, margins, and inventory value. For businesses with more than one location, branches, warehouses, and inter-branch transfers are managed from a single screen — each with its own settings, yet rolling up into one shared view.

And because a business platform is only as good as the trust placed in it, Taajir is built on role-based access control, detailed audit trails, and secure authentication — so owners can delegate freely while keeping full visibility into who did what, and when.

At a glance

  • — Inventory with variants, serials, barcodes, and multi-unit conversions
  • — Stock control across transfers, adjustments, and low-stock alerts
  • — Fast POS with quotations, returns, and VAT-aware invoicing
  • — Purchasing with supplier records, invoices, and returns
  • — Accounting with a live ledger, expenses, and multi-method payments
  • — Multi-branch locations, transfers, and per-branch settings
  • — Reporting with real-time dashboards and inventory valuation
  • — Security with role-based access, audit trails, and secure auth

The result is a calmer way to run a business: less time spent reconciling tools, and more spent on the decisions that actually grow it.
