Taajir Feature List

Comprehensive Inventory & Business Management System

Inventory Management

  • Multi-variant Products: Manage items with variations like size, color, and material.
  • Serial Tracking: Unique serial number tracking for electronics and high-value items.
  • Barcode System: Built-in barcode generation and registry for seamless scanning.
  • Stock Movements: Comprehensive tracking of inbound, outbound, transfers, and adjustments.
  • Low Stock Alerts: Automated notifications based on customizable stock thresholds.
  • Custom Fields: Add specific metadata for different product categories.

Sales & POS

  • POS Terminal: Optimized Point-of-Sale interface for quick retail transactions.
  • Quotations: Professional quotation generation for customers.
  • Order Management: Track sales orders from pending to delivered.
  • Sales Invoicing: Integrated VAT calculation, discount management, and multi-template support.
  • Sales Returns: Easy handling of customer returns and stock restoration.

Purchase & Suppliers

  • Supplier Directory: Centralized management of supplier contact and payment info.
  • Purchase Invoices: Record and track all incoming stock purchases.
  • Purchase Returns: Manage returns to suppliers with automated stock updates.
  • Procurement Tracking: Monitor lead times and supplier performance.

Financials & Accounting

  • Transaction Ledger: Real-time tracking of all inbound and outbound cash flows.
  • Expense Tracking: Categorize and monitor business expenses (rent, utilities, etc.).
  • Multi-Method Payments: Support for Cash, Bank, Card, and Mobile Banking accounts.
  • Payment Accounts: Manage multiple business accounts and reconciliations.

Multi-Branch Support

  • Location Management: Manage multiple branches and warehouses from a single dashboard.
  • Inter-Branch Transfers: Seamlessly move stock between different locations.
  • Branch Analytics: Compare performance across different business sites.
  • Localized Preferences: Branch-specific invoice headers and settings.

Security & Administration

  • RBAC: Robust Role-Based Access Control with fine-grained permissions.
  • Audit Trails: Detailed logs of every action performed in the system.
  • Organization Management: Control global settings, VAT rates, and business details.
  • Secure Auth: Email/Phone authentication with SSO capabilities.

Reporting & Insights

  • Dashboard Widgets: Real-time overview of sales, stock levels, and expenses.
  • Sales Reports: Detailed analysis of revenue and top-selling items.
  • Inventory Valuation: Understand the financial value of your current stock.
  • Audit Reports: Compliance and security reporting based on system logs.

Units & Conversions

  • Multi-Unit Support: Sell in pieces, boxes, kilograms, or custom units.
  • Conversion Rules: Automated math for moving between bulk and retail units.
  • Precision Control: Manage unit descriptions and symbols for professional invoicing.
