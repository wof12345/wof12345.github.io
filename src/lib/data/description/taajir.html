Taajir is an end-to-end inventory management and point-of-sale system built for small and medium businesses that need to track stock, sell, and analyze — without paying enterprise prices.

Designed from scratch to handle real-time data flow and offline-resilient operations at the counter.

  • Real-time stock tracking synced across devices via Supabase realtime channels.
  • Sales analytics broken down by product, category, and time window.
  • Customizable receipt printing, barcode workflows, and product variants.
  • Role-based access for cashiers, managers, and owners.

The stack pairs a Svelte 5 front-end with a FastAPI backend and PostgreSQL, with Vite handling the build.
