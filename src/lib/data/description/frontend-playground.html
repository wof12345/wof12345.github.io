Front-end Playground collects the smaller things I built while learning: layouts, templates, and experiments that each taught me one thing.

  • — Foodie and storefront templates: landing pages, product grids, carts, and multi-step sign-up forms in plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
  • — Physics sandbox: Three.js meshes driven by a cannon-es world, with orbit controls.
  • — Ad banners: fixed-size HTML display ads built to standard ad slot dimensions.
  • — Command palette: a keyboard-friendly search and quick-actions panel in SvelteKit and Tailwind.
  • — Face check: a webcam face-detection step with face-api.js that waits for a face to be centred in the frame.
  • — Portfolio and sign-in pages: early personal pages and login screens.
