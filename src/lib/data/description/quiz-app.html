Quiz App lets anyone write a quiz with a time limit and anyone else take it before the clock runs out.

The builder sets a title, an author, and a time limit, then adds and removes questions. Each quiz gets an ID, is saved in the browser, and appears in the list of available quizzes. Taking a quiz shows the questions with a countdown, and the result is shown at the end, or when time runs out.

The countdown runs in a Web Worker instead of on the page. Browsers slow down timers in background tabs, so a quiz timed on the main thread could give someone extra time just because they switched tabs. The worker keeps ticking at the right rate either way.
