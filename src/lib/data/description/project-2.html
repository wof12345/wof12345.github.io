A comprehensive merchant dashboard giving operators a bird’s-eye view of business performance, built around fast analytics and ergonomic data entry.

Headline features:

  • Real-time sales charts rendered with D3.js, updated as orders land.
  • Robust inventory management with batch editing across thousands of SKUs.
  • Customer CRM tracking purchase history and lifetime value.
  • Role-based permissions for staff, accounting, and owners.

Accessibility and responsiveness were first-class concerns from day one.
