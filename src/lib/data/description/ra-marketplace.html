RA Marketplace is a property marketplace where owners list homes to rent or sell and seekers find them, with an admin team approving what goes live.

There are two kinds of account. Providers create listings, track what they've rented out and sold, and manage their subscriptions. Seekers browse, search, and keep their personal details and subscription in one place. Admins review pending providers and listings, approve or reject them, and follow payments, rentals, and seekers from an overview. The front end is SvelteKit with OpenLayers maps for property locations. The back end is FastAPI with SQLAlchemy and Alembic migrations.

Approval before anything is public

Both providers and listings start as pending. Only after an admin approves them do they show up in search, which keeps fake listings out without slowing genuine providers down much. Subscriptions are stored with a full payment history, so the admin overview can show what each provider has paid and when.
