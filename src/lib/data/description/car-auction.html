JP Auction Center lets someone buying an imported Japanese car check it before they pay: type in the chassis number and see the car's auction record and sheet.

The public side is a single search box. Behind it, staff use a dashboard to add cars, upload their auction sheets and photos, generate QR codes that link straight to a car's page, and move each car through new, approved, and rejected. Users and their passwords are managed from the same dashboard. The front end is SvelteKit with Tailwind, and the back end is FastAPI with JWT auth and Alembic migrations.

Auction sheets on any screen

Auction sheets arrive as PDFs, which many phone browsers won't display inline. The details page renders the sheet as an image preview instead, so it reads the same on a phone at a dealership as on a desktop. Chassis numbers are unique, so the same car can't be entered twice.
