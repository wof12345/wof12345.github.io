Welcome to my new blog. I built this section of the site as a quiet place to write about engineering, the projects on my portfolio, and things I learn along the way.

## Why a blog?

I read a lot of other engineers' blogs and benefit enormously from them. Time to start giving back. The bar I'm setting for myself: write things I'd want to read.

## What to expect

- Notes on **Svelte 5**, runes, and the small UI patterns I keep reaching for
- Short post-mortems on the projects in my portfolio
- The occasional rabbit hole into databases, APIs, or tooling

If anything here is useful to you, that's the whole point.
